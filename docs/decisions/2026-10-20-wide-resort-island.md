Status: accepted.

Callum requested: "How might we expand Resort Island? As a first POC, could we take the middle portion of the map, make a slice, and then open up a plain flat space bridging the two slices?" Source: project conversation, 2026-10-20. He approved the plan's recommended answers: copies of the cut column fill the strip, and the widths are 8, 16, or 32 tiles with 16 as the default.

Widen the course after it loads rather than shipping new level files. `WideIsland_Apply` runs in `InitLevel` straight after `ParseTerrainHeader`. At that point the render mesh, the decorations, the collision mesh, the AI waypoints, the position tables, and the tile map are all in memory, and nothing has read them yet. `InitLevel` reloads every file for each race, so the change cannot apply twice. A cut sits on the playfield column boundary nearest X = 0, at X −16 on Resort Island. Every X east of it moves east by the strip width, and anything that crosses the cut stretches. Track parts, walls, and collision faces stretch, so the visible road and its collision stay in step. Decorations narrower than 1000 units move whole, so trees keep their shape. Rotating decorations move by their pivot. The tile map gains copies of the column west of the cut, so the sea stays sea and the lagoon, grass, and beach widen.

Keep the stock 32 by 32 collision grid and map each lookup back to the stock course with `WideIslandGridX`. A position in the strip maps to the cut, where the stock cell lists already hold every face that now crosses the strip. Rebuilding the grid would need a widened grid, and every lookup site hard-codes 32 cells. The grid's +X clamp grows by the strip width. A stretched collision surface no longer suits `PointInPolygon`, which rejects points against the extended lines of a strip's end edges and binary-searches the rest. A stretched edge can lie almost along the road and cut holes in it, and some stretched faces become concave. Surfaces that cross the strip therefore take a per-face test over the two triangles `InterpolateGroundHeight` already uses, and their faces get normals recomputed from the stretched corners. Every other surface keeps the stock test. The minimap also maps X through `WideIslandGridX`, so a racer in the strip shows on the cut line of the stock map art.

Apply only on Resort Island, outside demos and network sessions, and never to the title or option screens. Ghost loading, ghost saving, and replay saving are off whenever the current or next race is widened, the same way modern movement blocks them. Records written to those files describe the stock course. Android sends the strip width through JNI like the CPU opponents. The engine reads it at the next load, so a change during a race takes effect from the next Resort Island race.

The cost is stretched textures across the strip, since adding objects would break the hard-coded object indices that triggers and animations rely on. See [the guide and test list](../wide-island.md).
